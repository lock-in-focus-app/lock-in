01 · Tame, don't just block

Keep the site. Lose the feed.

Most blockers treat a site as all or nothing. Lock In gives every site one of four levels, chosen per profile: one for writing, another for winding down.

Built in for Reddit, YouTube, X, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Twitch, Threads and Bluesky. Add any other site by its address, and click away any part of a page you'd rather not see.

02 · A pause before the scroll

Five minutes first.

A redirected site opens a calm page instead. Focus for five minutes; if you still want the site after that, go right ahead, for a few minutes. Your own if-then plan is right there, and so are the links you meant to open.

Saying no is always one tap: Not now takes you back to what you were doing.

03 · The desktop app

Your focus fills a night sky.

The extension works on its own. The app for macOS and Linux adds a sky that grows as you focus, drawn by code and kept on your computer.

A star for every session

Longer sessions make bigger, brighter stars, in your profile's color.

A constellation every week

Each week's stars join into a shape that's yours alone, with a name you can change.

A planet every month

It takes shape as the hours add up: a rock, then round, then rings and an atmosphere.

Reply to Sam later

Satellites for stray thoughts

Alt+P from anywhere puts a thought in orbit. It comes back when the session ends.

  • Close or hide apps like Discord or Steam during a session
  • Gentle, Medium or Hard sessions
  • A quiet timer that doesn't cut you off
  • A weekly report

Missed days cost nothing. The sky never loses a star, so there's no streak to break.

04 · Why it works

Built on research that has held up.

Each feature is checked against the research behind it, and we say how strong that research is.

Modest · one study

A pause and an easy way out cut how often you give in.

In a six-week field study, openings of the chosen apps fell 57%. The way out mattered most; the wait helped.

Grüning et al., 2023 · the redirect page
Strong · large field experiment

Limits you set yourself work.

Letting people cap their own future screen time cut their social media use a lot.

Allcott, Gentzkow & Song, 2022 · profiles and Hard mode
Strong · 94 tests

If-then plans help you follow through.

Deciding ahead "when X happens, I'll do Y" had a medium-to-large effect on reaching goals.

Gollwitzer & Sheeran, 2006 · your plan on the redirect page

05 · Private by design

Nothing leaves your computer.

Your profiles, sessions and sky stay on your device. When the app and the extension are both installed, they talk over your browser's Native Messaging, which never leaves your computer either.

  • No accountsNothing to sign up for.
  • No analytics, no adsNothing is counted or sold.
  • Open sourceThe code is public, so you can check any of it.
