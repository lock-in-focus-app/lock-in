02 · The redirect page

A pause before the scroll

Visits to a redirected site go to a calm Lock In page first. Focus for five minutes, read your own if-then plan, or open one of your links. If you still need the site once the wait is up, take a few minutes on it. Or don't: Not now is always right there.

Why a pause works. In a six-week field study of a pause-before-opening app, people opened the apps they chose 57% less. The easy way out mattered most; the wait helped too. (Grüning et al., 2023. One study, so a modest finding.)

03 · The desktop app

A night sky that fills with your focus

Outside a session, the app's sky is washed out by haze. When you lock in, it clears, and your new star forms.

  • Stars

    Every session becomes a star in your profile's color. Longer sessions, bigger and brighter stars.

  • Constellations

    Each week's stars join into a constellation with a made-up name, which you can rename.

  • Planets

    Each month with focus grows a planet that takes shape as the hours add up. No two are alike.

  • Moons

    Days with focus in a row earn moons around the month's planet. A missed day never takes one away.

Missed days cost nothing: the sky never loses anything. Comets, meteor showers and auroras turn up now and then, and nothing pops up to tell you.

04 · Satellites · thoughts for later

Launch the thought. Keep the focus.

Mid-focus thoughts like "I should check this real quick" turn into distractions. Press Alt+P (Option+P on a Mac) from anywhere, type it, and press Return. It goes into orbit, and you're straight back where you were.

  • Out of sight

    During a session you only see how many are in orbit, not what they say.

  • Back when you're done

    Nothing pops up. When the session ends, they wait for you: "3 satellites came back."

  • A quick checklist

    Tick each one Done, Snooze it into orbit again, make it a Task, or Delete it.

Why it works. Writing down a plan for an unfinished task stops it from pulling at your attention. (Masicampo & Baumeister, 2011. A set of lab studies, so a modest finding.)

    05 · Private by design

    Nothing leaves your computer

    Lock In collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own.

    • Kept on your device

      Your profiles, sessions, history and sky stay in your browser and the app's data folder.

    • Linked locally

      The extension and the app talk over your browser's Native Messaging, which never leaves your computer.

    • No network at all

      Neither makes network requests. The source code is public, so you can check any of it.
