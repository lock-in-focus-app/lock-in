✦ Lock In

Website mock-ups

Three takes on the site from PLAN §10: a light-polluted sky full of distractions that clears when you lock in. Same colors, frames and copy in each; they differ in layout and motion. They aren't the site yet, just something to react to. Each one respects reduced motion.

  1. A

    The switch

    PLAN §10 as written. A big Lock In switch in the middle of a noisy, glowing sky. Flip it and the distractions spring away, the orange drains out and a constellation draws itself. Below, a calm scroll where every section shows distracted, then focused.

  2. B

    Observatory

    After your reference image. One framed screen with a huge glowing wordmark over drifting nebulae and readouts in the corners. A constellation along the bottom is the navigation: each section is a star that lights up as you reach it.

  3. C

    Drift

    Scrolling flies you forward through floating feeds and notifications, which puff out as you pass, into a clear sky. Then the real screens float on glass cards that tilt under the pointer, with a try-it-yourself demo of the four levels.
