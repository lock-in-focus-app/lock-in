A logo, and a little Japanese

Seiza is 星座, a constellation, and sounds the same as 正座, the calm kneeling posture. Written in hiragana, せいざ could be either, so it carries both meanings at once. Six logo ideas first, each as an app icon and at favicon sizes, then places the Japanese could go.

    Japanese touches

    A light hand: one or two of these, not all. The kanji 星座 says constellation outright; the hiragana せいざ is the softer reading and keeps the double meaning.

    • Seiza せいざ

      Under the mark

      The website's nav mark with its reading in place of the gold underline. Small and always there.

      Down the frame's edge

      On the website, 星座 written top to bottom along the frame's right side, faint lavender, like the side of a poster. The one I'd pick.

      A gloss in a corner

      A readout in the website's bottom-left corner, or the footer, saying what the name means. It could also mention 正座, sitting calmly.

      夜空 (yozora, the night sky) beside the Sky section's label. Only there, so it stays special.
